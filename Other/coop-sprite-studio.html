Unique mechanics

Unique behavior is the item's own JavaScript hook module. Hooks include onUse, onHit, onBlockBreak, onBlockPlace, and onTick. They receive the event, player, nearby players, and ray-trace context.

Shared JavaScript services

Unique hooks call a service with api.services.burst(count). The active hook's API and event are passed to the service automatically. Services are separated from item behavior so one helper can be reused by every hook on that item. Scripts require player approval; game-changing actions remain validated by the game/server.
